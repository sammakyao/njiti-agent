Whatever you need to know about caring for a baby born too early, start here.

Try asking

Go to a health facility now if your baby:

  • is not breathing, or is struggling to breathe
  • has blue lips, tongue or skin
  • has fits (convulsions)
  • stops feeding or cannot suck
  • feels very cold or very hot
  • is very sleepy, floppy or hard to wake
  • has yellow palms or soles
  • has a red, swollen or smelly umbilical cord
